The repo's own code.js runs here against a small figma stub, and ui.html runs in a sandboxed iframe (null origin, as in Figma) with the fallback palette injected the way showUI({ themeColors: true }) does. Saved sizes go to this browser's localStorage. Add ?config={"snapWidths":[300,400]} to the address to try a data-kit-config override.
